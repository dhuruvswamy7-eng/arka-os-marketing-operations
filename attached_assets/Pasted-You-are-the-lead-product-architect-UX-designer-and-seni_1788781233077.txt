You are the lead product architect, UX designer, and senior full-stack engineer for ARKA OS.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT build another generic project-management dashboard.
Do NOT recreate the existing marketing/content-management style application.
Do NOT make ARKA OS primarily about campaigns, content calendars, scheduled reach, content production, approvals, or social media.

The existing generated app is conceptually wrong.

ARKA OS is an INTERNAL OPERATING SYSTEM for Arka Digital Media.

Its purpose is to give the founder and management a live operational understanding of:

PEOPLE
→ WORK
→ ASSIGNMENT
→ DEADLINE
→ EXECUTION
→ PROGRESS
→ TIME / EFFORT

5. WORK LIFECYCLE
==================================================

The work lifecycle is one of the most important parts of ARKA OS.

Default lifecycle:

1. INBOX
2. PLANNING
3. ASSIGNED
4. IN PROGRESS
5. REVIEW
6. REVISION
7. APPROVED
8. COMPLETED
9. BLOCKED

A work item can move between stages.

Every stage transition must be recorded.

For every transition capture:

- previous stage
- new stage
- changed by
- timestamp
- optional note

This creates a real operational history.

Website Landing Page

↓

The system should be able to later calculate:

- total cycle time
- number of revisions
- time waiting for review
- time blocked
- actual effort
- delay against deadline

This is what makes ARKA OS an operating system rather than a checklist application.

Planning
Assigned
In Progress
Review
Revision
Approved
Completed

STATUS represents operational condition:

Active
Blocked
Completed
Cancelled

A work item can therefore be:

Stage = In Progress
Status = Active

or:

Stage = In Progress
Status = Blocked

This distinction is important for meaningful reporting.

Not Asana clone.

Not Trello clone.

Not a CRM.

THE OPERATING LAYER OF ARKA DIGITAL MEDIA.
